## Where the criteria live

Criteria are in "Coverage Indications, Limitations, and/or Medical Necessity":
"Covered Indications" (Total knee arthroplasty (TKA)) and "Limitations". The LCD
says A59811 holds the documentation requirements. The LCD also covers THA and
revisions; only primary TKA is modeled here.

## Conditions for coverage (primary TKA)

1. **Indication (TKA-1, Covered Indications) — at least ONE of:** failure of
   previous osteotomy; distal femur fracture; proximal tibia fracture; malignancy of
   the distal femur, proximal tibia, knee joint or adjacent soft tissues; avascular
   necrosis of the knee; failed previous unicompartmental joint replacement
   (Limitations); or **advanced joint disease demonstrated by all of TKA-2, TKA-3,
   and TKA-4**.
2. **Imaging (TKA-2, Covered Indications; A59811) — at least ONE finding:**
   radiographic evidence (or MRI and/or CT when radiography is not adequate) of
   subchondral cysts, subchondral sclerosis, periarticular osteophytes, joint
   subluxation, joint space narrowing, or avascular necrosis.
3. **Pain or function (TKA-3, Covered Indications; A59811).** Pain or functional
   disability attributable to the advanced joint disease. A59811 asks for the extent
   to which it interferes with ADLs, increases with activity, or increases with
   weight bearing.
4. **Failed conservative therapy (TKA-4, Covered Indications; A59811).** An
   unsuccessful history of appropriate non-surgical management, clearly addressed in
   the pre-procedure record, usually for 3 months or more. It typically includes 1 or
   more of: anti-inflammatory medications; analgesics; flexibility and muscle
   strengthening exercises; supervised physical therapy (ADLs diminished despite
   completing a plan of care); activity restrictions as is reasonable; assistive
   device use; weight reduction as appropriate; therapeutic knee injections as
   appropriate. If non-surgical care is not appropriate, the record must state why.
5. **No contraindication (TKA-5, Limitations).** None of: active infection of the
   hip or knee joint or active systemic bacteremia; active skin infection or open
   wound within the planned surgical site; neuropathic arthritis; rapidly
   progressing neurological disease.
6. **Procedure and device (TKA-6, Limitations).** A total knee arthroplasty, not a
   unicompartmental replacement. FDA class II or III implant under 21 CFR Part 888.

## Denial rule (Limitations)

"TKA or THA is not considered medically reasonable and necessary when any of the
above criteria are not met." It is also not reasonable and necessary when a listed
contraindication is present.

## Documentation notes (A59811)

- For significant comorbidities (for example coronary artery disease or obstructive
  pulmonary disease), the record should address the risk/benefit of TKA.
- Non-surgical care may be inappropriate with bone on bone articulation, severe
  deformity, or severe pain (particularly at rest) with disabling ADL interference.

## Demo PA approval checklist (derived)

A clean APPROVAL demo scenario (member in Michigan, J8):

| # | Criterion | Evidence in packet |
|---|---|---|
| TKA-1 | Advanced joint disease path | Right knee primary osteoarthritis (ICD-10-CM M17.11 on A59811 covered list) |
| TKA-2 | Imaging finding | Standing radiographs: severe medial joint space narrowing (bone on bone), periarticular osteophytes, subchondral sclerosis and cysts |
| TKA-3 | Pain or functional disability | Pain 8/10 on weight bearing; walks 1 block; needs rail for stairs; help getting in and out of tub |
| TKA-4 | Failed conservative therapy (>= 3 months) | 8 months meloxicam and acetaminophen; 12 supervised PT visits completed with ADLs still limited; cane; 12 lb weight loss; 2 corticosteroid injections with short relief |
| TKA-5 | No contraindication | Skin intact; no infection; no neuropathic arthropathy; no progressive neurologic disease |
| TKA-6 | Total knee, FDA device | Planned right TKA (CPT 27447) with an FDA-cleared tricompartmental implant |

TKA-1, TKA-2, and TKA-4 are ANY-ONE-OF criteria. TKA-2 to TKA-4 apply to the
advanced joint disease path and must all be met on that path. The rubric engine uses
OR-logic within those criteria and AND-logic across TKA-1 to TKA-6.
