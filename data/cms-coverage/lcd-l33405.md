## Where the criteria live

All criteria are in "Coverage Indications, Limitations, and/or Medical Necessity"
(CILMN): History/Background (device types), Covered Indications "1. Sleep Apnea",
Limitations, Provider Qualifications, and Facility Accreditation. "Associated
Information" points to A57496 for documentation requirements and utilization
guidelines.

Item A of "1. Sleep Apnea" sits under "For a diagnosis of OSA to be made, the
following criteria must be met" and starts with "Prior to sleep testing". It is a
pre-service requirement. Item B (AHI/RDI thresholds) applies to the test result, so
it is not a prior authorization criterion.

## Conditions for coverage (attended Type I PSG for suspected OSA)

All of the following must be met:

1. **Indication (PSG-1, History/Background; 1. Sleep Apnea).** The beneficiary has
   clinical signs and symptoms indicative of OSA, and the PSG aids the diagnosis of
   OSA.
2. **Sleep history (PSG-2, 1. Sleep Apnea, item A).** Before testing, a
   face-to-face evaluation by the treating physician documents sleep history and
   symptoms, including but not limited to snoring, daytime sleepiness, observed
   apneas, choking or gasping during sleep, and morning headaches.
3. **Epworth Sleepiness Scale (PSG-3, 1. Sleep Apnea, item A; Limitations).** The
   pre-test evaluation includes an Epworth Sleepiness Scale.
4. **Physical exam (PSG-4, 1. Sleep Apnea, item A).** The pre-test exam documents
   body mass index, neck circumference, and a focused cardiopulmonary and upper
   airway system evaluation.
5. **Attended in-lab setting (PSG-5, History/Background: Type I).** Performed
   attended in a sleep laboratory. A technologist is physically present and able to
   intervene. Trained personnel are in constant attendance. Minimum channels: EEG,
   EOG, chin EMG, anterior tibialis EMG, ECG, airflow, respiratory effort, oxygen
   saturation; body position documented or measured.
6. **Interpreting physician (PSG-6, Provider Qualifications) — at least ONE of:**
   ABSM or ABMS Diplomate; ABMS member-board sleep certification; AOA member-board
   sleep certification; active staff of an AASM-accredited sleep center or lab;
   active staff of a Joint Commission-accredited sleep lab; active staff of an
   ACHC-accredited sleep lab; ABFM Diplomate with CAQ in Sleep Medicine.
7. **Facility and technologist (PSG-7, Sleep Technologist/Technician; Facility
   Accreditation).** Facility accredited by AASM, ACHC, or the Joint Commission, with
   documentation on file. Attending technologists hold a credential (for example
   RPSGT, R. EEG T. - Polysomnography, CRT-SDS, RRT-SDS, RST), or are affiliated
   with an accredited facility and supervised by a registered technologist.

## Limitations and non-coverage

- HST is not intended for patients with comorbidities: congestive heart failure,
  hypo-ventilation syndrome, moderate to severe pulmonary disease, or neuromuscular
  disease. (This supports in-lab testing. It is not a documentation requirement for
  PSG.)
- "The routine use of a two test routine (HST followed by PSG) to diagnose sleep
  apnea would not be considered reasonable and necessary." A repeat PSG must be
  clearly supported in the record.
- Not indicated for chronic insomnia (unless sleep apnea is a suspected
  contributor). Circadian rhythm disorder testing is not covered.
- Not routinely indicated for restless legs syndrome, or for epilepsy without sleep
  complaints. Not indicated to diagnose chronic lung disease.
- PSG or HST for a noncovered oral appliance is not covered.
