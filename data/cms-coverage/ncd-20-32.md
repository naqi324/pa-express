## Covered Indication (Section B.A — FDA-approved indication pathway)

TAVR is covered for the treatment of **symptomatic aortic valve stenosis** when furnished
according to an FDA-approved indication and when **all** of the following conditions are met:

1. **FDA-approved system (TAVR-2).** The procedure is furnished with a complete aortic valve and
   implantation system that has received FDA premarket approval (PMA) for that system's
   FDA-approved indication.
2. **Heart team (TAVR-3).** The patient (pre- and post-operatively) is under the care of a
   cohesive, multidisciplinary heart team that includes a cardiac surgeon and an interventional
   cardiologist experienced in the care and treatment of aortic stenosis, plus other physician
   groups, advanced practice practitioners, nurses, research personnel, and administrators.
3. **Independent dual evaluation (TAVR-4).** The cardiac surgeon and interventional cardiologist
   have each independently examined the patient face-to-face, evaluated the patient's suitability
   for SAVR, TAVR, or medical/palliative therapy, and documented and shared the rationale for
   their clinical judgment with the heart team.
4. **Joint intra-operative participation (TAVR-5).** The heart team's interventional
   cardiologist(s) and cardiac surgeon(s) must jointly participate in the intra-operative
   technical aspects of TAVR.
5. **Hospital infrastructure (TAVR-6).** TAVR is furnished in a hospital with appropriate
   infrastructure, including on-site heart valve surgery and interventional cardiology programs
   and a post-procedure intensive care facility with personnel experienced in managing open-heart
   valve patients.
6. **Volume qualifications (TAVR-7).**
   - *Programs with TAVR experience must maintain:* >= 50 AVRs (TAVR or SAVR) per year including
     >= 20 TAVR procedures in the prior year, **or** >= 100 AVRs every 2 years including >= 40
     TAVR procedures in the prior 2 years; **and** >= 2 physicians with cardiac surgery
     privileges; **and** >= 1 physician with interventional cardiology privileges; **and**
     >= 300 PCIs per year.
   - *Programs/heart teams without TAVR experience* have separate start-up qualifications
     (>= 50 open heart surgeries in prior year; >= 20 aortic valve procedures in prior 2 years;
     surgeon with >= 100 career open-heart / >= 25 aortic valve cases; interventional
     cardiologist with >= 100 career structural or >= 30 left-sided structural procedures/year
     plus device-specific training).
7. **Registry participation (TAVR-8).** The heart team and hospital participate in a prospective,
   national, audited registry (in practice, the STS/ACC TVT Registry) that consecutively enrolls
   TAVR patients, accepts all manufactured devices, follows patients for at least one year, and
   complies with 45 CFR Part 46 and 21 CFR Parts 50 & 56. Tracked outcomes: stroke; all-cause
   mortality; TIAs; major vascular events; acute kidney injury; repeat aortic valve procedures;
   new permanent pacemaker implantation; quality of life.

## Section B.B — Non-FDA-approved uses

Covered only when performed within a qualifying clinical study meeting CED standards
(quality-of-life evaluation pre/post TAVR for a minimum of 1 year plus at least one specified
research question, and 13 standards of scientific integrity). Not relevant to a standard PA
approval demo.

## Nationally Non-Covered (Section C)

> TAVR is not covered for patients in whom existing co-morbidities would preclude the expected
> benefit from correction of the aortic stenosis.

| # | Criterion | Typical evidence in a PA packet |

| TAVR-1 | Symptomatic severe aortic stenosis | Echo report (valve area <= 1.0 cm2, mean gradient >= 40 mmHg or peak velocity >= 4.0 m/s), documented NYHA class II-IV symptoms, ICD-10 I35.0 |

| TAVR-3 | Multidisciplinary heart team | Heart team conference note |
| TAVR-4 | Independent face-to-face evals by CT surgeon + interventional cardiologist with documented rationale | Two consult notes; STS risk score documented |
| TAVR-5 | Joint intra-operative participation | Attestation in operative plan |
| TAVR-6 | Hospital infrastructure | Facility attestation |
| TAVR-7 | Volume qualifications | Facility volume attestation |
| TAVR-8 | TVT Registry participation | Registry participation attestation |
| Exclusion | Co-morbidities do not preclude benefit | Heart team documentation of expected benefit / life expectancy |
