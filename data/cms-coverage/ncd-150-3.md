## Conditions for coverage (42 CFR 410.31)

All of the following must be met:

1. **Ordering (BMD-1, 410.31(b)(1)(i)).** The BMM is ordered by the physician or qualified
   nonphysician practitioner treating the beneficiary, following an evaluation of the need for
   the measurement and a determination of the medically appropriate procedure.
2. **Technical (BMD-2, 410.31(a)).** Performed for identifying bone mass / detecting bone loss /
   determining bone quality, with an FDA-cleared or FDA-approved bone densitometer (other than
   single-photon or dual-photon absorptiometry) or bone sonometer, and includes a physician's
   interpretation of the results.
3. **Supervision (BMD-3, 410.31(b)(1)(ii)).** Performed under the appropriate level of physician
   supervision per 42 CFR 410.32(b).
4. **Qualifying beneficiary category (BMD-4, 410.31(d)) — at least ONE of:**
   1. A woman determined by her treating physician/qualified NPP to be **estrogen-deficient and at
      clinical risk for osteoporosis**, based on medical history and other findings.
   2. An individual with **vertebral abnormalities** demonstrated by x-ray indicative of
      osteoporosis, osteopenia, or vertebral fracture.
   3. An individual receiving (or expecting to receive) **glucocorticoid therapy** equivalent to an
      average of >= 5.0 mg prednisone/day for more than 3 months.
   4. An individual with **primary hyperparathyroidism**.
   5. An individual being **monitored to assess response to or efficacy of an FDA-approved
      osteoporosis drug therapy** (monitoring must use central/axial DXA per 410.31(b)(2)).
5. **Frequency (BMD-5, 410.31(c)).** At least 23 months have passed since the month of the last
   covered BMM, **unless** a medical-necessity exception applies. Named examples:
   - monitoring beneficiaries on long-term glucocorticoid therapy of more than 3 months;
   - a confirmatory baseline central DXA to permit future monitoring when the initial measurement
     was performed with a different modality (410.31(b)(3)).

## Denial rule (410.31(e))

If the conditions of coverage (b), qualifying categories (d), or frequency standards (c) are not
met, the service is excluded from Medicare coverage as not "reasonable and necessary" under
section 1862(a)(1)(A) of the Act.

this criterion while requiring AND-logic across BMD-1..BMD-5.
